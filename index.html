Top 10 Most Visited Places in India

1. Taj Mahal, Agra

An ivory-white marble mausoleum and UNESCO World Heritage Site.

2. Jaipur, Rajasthan

The Pink City, known for its majestic forts and palaces.

3. Goa

Famous for beaches, nightlife and Portuguese heritage.

4. Kerala Backwaters

Serene houseboat cruises through lush tropical waterways.

5. Varanasi

One of the world's oldest living cities, sacred to Hindus.

6. Leh-Ladakh

Breathtaking Himalayan landscapes and high-altitude lakes.

7. Amritsar

Home to the Golden Temple, the spiritual heart of Sikhism.

8. Udaipur

The City of Lakes, famous for its romantic palaces.

9. Rishikesh

The Yoga Capital of the World, in the Himalayan foothills.

10. Andaman Islands

Turquoise waters, white sand and vibrant coral reefs.
